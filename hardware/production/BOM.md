## Assembly

1. Press the magnet into the base underside (flush). Screw the panel socket into the pod.
2. Pan servo into the base (two M2 × 8 through its tabs); slide the pan cap over the housing; two M2 × 8 into its sides.
3. Pan horn (centred) into the yoke pocket; press the yoke onto the pan spline; horn screw from above.
4. Tilt servo through the yoke's right upright; slide the shroud over it; two M2 × 8 through the shroud and tabs.
5. Enclosure: battery → tray (clicks in) → board with heatsink (onto the posts) → antenna sticker on the far wall.
6. Light pipe into the lid; lid on; two M2 × 8.
7. Trim the tilt horn to 16 mm; seat it in the drive cheek; set both servos to 90° before fixing the horns.
8. Enclosure between the uprights: horn onto the tilt spline, M2 × 8 pivot through the left upright.
9. Cable: right-angle plug into the board, through the strain clip, upright clip, pan-loop clip, into the pod.
10. Wiring: servo signals to D0/D1, servo 5 V/GND to the XIAO 5V/GND with the capacitor across them.
