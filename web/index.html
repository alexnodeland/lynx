01 · power

One USB-C cable.

Power and data go in through the base. Inside, a braided lead rides three clips and a spring relief, so nothing tugs on the board when it moves.

02 · storage

microSD, right where it should be.

The slot sits above the USB-C port with a clear path in — checked in CAD, not hoped for.

03 · motion

±90° pan. ±60° tilt.

Two MG90S servos on D0 and D1. Moves ease and settle like a head turn, then the PWM switches off so it doesn't hum.
